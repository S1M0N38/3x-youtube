## 3xYouTube Browser Extension Privacy Policy

***Last Updated: February 2025***
<br/>

## 1. Information Collection

The ***3xYouTube*** extension does ***not*** collect, store, or transmit any
personal data. It operates ***entirely locally*** on your device.
<br/>

## 2. Data Usage

* The extension uses the ***storage API*** for one value only: the playback
  speed you selected, so it can be re-applied to the next video.
* That value never leaves your browser and is not shared with anyone.
* The extension does ***not*** use analytics, tracking, or third-party
  services, and it makes no network requests of its own.
<br/>

## 3. Permissions Justification

* `storage` → Remembers your playback speed between videos and restarts.
* `*://www.youtube.com/*` → Required to change the playback rate of the video
  element and to add the speed control to the player's settings menu.

The extension runs only on `youtube.com`; it has no access to other sites, no
toolbar button, and no background network activity beyond the keyboard
shortcut listener.
